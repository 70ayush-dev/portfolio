# Portfolio homepage and case studies

Mode: Experience on the homepage; Read on case studies. Implement the user-approved design documents directly. Preserve product data, search capability content, project routes and contact destinations. No image mockup round requested.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Engineering Fieldnotes makes platform architecture inspectable through generous editorial composition. Open capability rows and varied project stories replace repeated icon-card scaffolds.

OWN-WORLD: warm paper, deep ink, restrained cobalt, Space Grotesk headings and Source Sans 3 prose. Native controls, compact technology labels and clear vector diagrams.

STORY: Meet Ayush, inspect connected CMS/backend/frontend systems, explore project decisions, discover search visibility services, then start a conversation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a compact masthead; an oversized three-line statement occupying eight columns; an unboxed interactive architecture annotation occupying four. Real name/role sits in the masthead. The primary selected-work action sits below the hero explanation. No moving background.

FORM: user-pinned Engineering Fieldnotes, from DESIGN.md. No seed: the visual world and composition are explicitly specified rather than open to concept selection. Signature interaction: selecting a CMS, API, frontend or AI node reveals its role, with keyboard support.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Authorized theme extension

User requested dark mode and animated theme switching during implementation, then specified a pure AMOLED black canvas (#000000). The header toggle persists preference, uses system preference when none is saved, and provides a 280ms View Transition fade with a 240ms color fallback. Reduced motion removes transitions. Dark colors are recorded in the final design system.
